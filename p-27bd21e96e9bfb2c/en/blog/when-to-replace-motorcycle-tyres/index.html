When should you replace your motorcycle tyres?

Replace your motorcycle tyres when the tread has worn down to the wear indicators (legal minimum in the Netherlands: 1 mm), when the rubber is old or cracked, or with a cut or bulge. The DOT code tells you how old your tyre is. Tyre fitting costs €50 per tyre with us, the tyre itself separately.

By Freddy · 20 October 2026

How much tread does a motorcycle tyre need?

In the Netherlands the legal minimum tread for a motorcycle tyre is 1 mm. That really is the minimum: with less tread your braking distance on wet roads gets longer. Many motorcycle tyres have wear indicators in the grooves. Once the tread has worn down to them, it is time for new tyres. Measure in several places, because motorcycle tyres often wear unevenly.

How old is your tyre according to the DOT code?

Even a tyre with enough tread can be too old: the rubber hardens and gives less grip. On the sidewall of your tyre is the DOT code. The last four digits give the production week and year. 2423, for example, means week 24 of 2023.

Besides age, look at the state of the rubber: small cracks in the sidewalls or between the tread are a sign the tyre needs replacing.

When is damage a reason to replace?

  • A cut, bulge or sidewall damage.
  • A nail or stone pushed into the tread.
  • A tyre worn flat or squared off in the middle, so your bike turns in less easily.
