Blog

On our blog we answer the questions riders ask us most often: what a service costs, how often your bike needs one, when to replace your tyres and what to do when something goes wrong. All written by Freddy, from our workshop in Waalwijk.

Which articles do we have?

  • What does a major or minor motorcycle service cost?
    There is no single fixed price for a service: it depends on your bike, the schedule and the parts.
  • Major or minor service: what is the difference?
    Minor service, major service: what actually happens? Below you see what we check in each service and when which service is due.
  • How many km between motorcycle services?
    The short answer: your manufacturer's service schedule tells you.
  • Motorcycle stalls when warm: possible causes
    Cold it runs fine, but once warm it stalls at idle or even while riding.
  • Motorcycle leaking oil or fuel: what now?
    A spot under your bike after it has stood still for a while: is it oil or fuel, and can you still ride it? Here is how to deal with it.
  • When should you replace your motorcycle tyres?
    Tread, age and damage: three reasons to replace your motorcycle tyres.
  • Motorcycle diagnostics, also on a BMW without a dealer
    A light on your dashboard or a bike that behaves strangely: a diagnostic check helps find the cause.
  • Buying a used motorcycle: what to look for?
    With a used motorcycle you first check the papers and service history, then tyres, brakes, chain, leaks and how it starts from cold.
  • How old can motorcycle tyres be?
    In the Netherlands there is no legal age limit for motorcycle tyres; there is one for tread (1 mm).
  • Get your motorcycle winter-ready: how to do it
    Not riding much in winter? Connect the battery to a trickle charger, fill the tank with a fuel stabiliser, lubricate the chain and set the tyre pressure.
