Annual motorcycle service in Waalwijk

Even if you ride little, a yearly service is wise: oil, brake fluid and tyres also age when your bike stands still. In the annual service we check your brakes, tyres, chain, lights and fluids following your manufacturer's schedule. Labour costs €75 per hour incl. VAT.

Why a service every year?

Even if you ride few kilometres, a yearly service is wise: oil, brake fluid and tyres also age when your bike stands still. With a service that follows your manufacturer's schedule, you know the important parts are checked regularly.

What do we check?

  • Brakes: pads, discs and brake fluid.
  • Tyres: tread, pressure, age and damage.
  • Chain and sprockets: wear, tension and lubrication.
  • Lights, battery and controls: everything you need to ride safely.
  • Engine oil and oil filter, with oil that suits your bike.
  • Fluids and filters, such as coolant and air filter, according to the schedule.

Minor or major service?

Which service your bike needs depends on the mileage and the service schedule. Read more about servicing and the major service. Not sure? Send us your mileage and we'll look it up for you.

What if we find something during the work?

If we spot something that needs attention during the service, we discuss it with you first. We tell you what really needs doing and what can wait a little.

What does it cost?

The total price depends on your bike, the schedule and which parts are needed. Message us your make, model and mileage, and you'll know up front where you stand.

We help riders from Waalwijk and the region, up to about 50 km from the workshop: from Sprang-Capelle, Kaatsheuvel and Drunen to Tilburg, 's-Hertogenbosch, Oosterhout and Breda.

Frequently asked questions about the annual service

Why a service every year?

Oil, brake fluid and tyres age even if you ride little. A yearly service keeps your bike reliable and safe.

What do you check?

Brakes, tyres, chain and sprockets, lights and leaks. A service following your manufacturer's schedule checks these on time.

How often does my bike need a service?

Your manufacturer's schedule tells you, in kilometres and in time, whichever comes first. Read how many km between services.

I want to buy a used motorcycle. Can you inspect it?

Yes, with a pre-purchase inspection. It costs €75 per inspection.
