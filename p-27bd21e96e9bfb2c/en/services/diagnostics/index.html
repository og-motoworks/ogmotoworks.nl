Motorcycle diagnostics and fault finding in Waalwijk

Is a warning light on, does your bike run rough or start badly? We connect the diagnostic tool where possible and then measure ourselves, so we find the real cause before any parts get replaced. You pay for the time: labour costs €75 per hour incl. VAT.

When should you have your bike diagnosed?

  • A warning or fault light is on.
  • Your bike runs rough, hesitates or stalls.
  • It starts badly or not at all.
  • Something electrical is acting strange: dashboard, lights or charging.
  • A fault keeps coming back, even after earlier repairs.

How do we tackle it?

We connect a diagnostic tool and look at the fault codes. Then we measure and check things ourselves, because a fault code doesn't always tell you where the problem really is. Only when it's clear what's going on do we discuss the solution with you.

Sometimes reading out and resetting is enough. On a BMW R1200GS Adventure the dashboard no longer showed neutral: after reading it out, resetting the gear values and re-teaching the gear positions, everything worked again.

What does it cost?

You pay for the time the diagnosis takes. We keep you updated, so you know where you stand.

We help riders from Waalwijk and the region, up to about 50 km from the workshop: from Sprang-Capelle, Kaatsheuvel and Drunen to Tilburg, 's-Hertogenbosch, Oosterhout and Breda.

Frequently asked questions about diagnostics

When should I have my bike diagnosed?

When a warning light is on, your bike runs unevenly, hesitates, stalls or starts badly, or when a fault keeps coming back.

What do diagnostics and fault finding cost?

You pay for the time: labour costs €75 per hour, incl. VAT. We keep you informed, so you know where you stand.

Can you run diagnostics on a BMW without a dealer?

Yes, we also run diagnostics on BMW motorcycles. We tell you in advance whether it is possible for your make, model and year. Read motorcycle diagnostics.

Is a fault code the solution straight away?

Not always. A fault code points to a system or sensor. We then measure and check ourselves, before any parts are replaced.
