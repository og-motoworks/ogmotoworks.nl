Custom work & lights

Make your bike your own. Tell us which lights or accessories you want fitted.

What we fit for you

  • Aftermarket indicators, for example smaller or LED indicators.
  • Aftermarket lights, such as a different headlight or tail light.
  • Neat wiring: cables tucked away and properly secured.
  • Does everything work? After fitting we check the lights and indicators.

LED indicators

LED indicators use much less power than regular bulbs. Because of that, on some bikes they flash too fast or don't work properly. We look at what your bike needs, so they flash normally again.

Road legal?

Choose lights and indicators with an E-mark. Not sure whether something fits or is allowed? Message us what you want and we'll think along.

Cost

The cost depends on your bike and what you want fitted. We charge parts at list price and labour at our hourly rate. Message us and you'll hear up front what to expect.
