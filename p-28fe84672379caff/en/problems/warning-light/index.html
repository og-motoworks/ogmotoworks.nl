Warning light on?

Is a light on on your dashboard? A red light, such as oil or temperature, means: stop safely and switch off the engine. An orange fault light usually means you can ride on carefully, but have it diagnosed soon. We run diagnostics in Waalwijk; labour costs €75 per hour incl. VAT.

Which light is it?

  • Red oil pressure light: stop as soon as it's safe and switch off the engine. Don't ride on until the cause is known.
  • Temperature light: let the engine cool down and check whether coolant is leaking or low.
  • Engine warning light: a fault code has been stored. If the bike runs normally, you can usually ride home or to us calmly.
  • ABS or traction control light: you can still brake, but the assistance systems may not work. Ride carefully.
  • Battery or charging light: the battery may not be charging. Chances are your bike won't start next time.

When in doubt, check your owner's manual: it explains what each light means.

What do we do?

We read your bike's fault memory and look at which fault codes are stored. Then we check things ourselves, because a fault code doesn't always point to the faulty part. Read more under diagnostics & faults.

Worried about safety? Then don't ride any further. Message us and we'll think along about the best solution.

Frequently asked questions

A red light is on. Can I keep riding?

No. A red light, such as oil or temperature, means: stop safely and switch off the engine. Message us and we'll tell you what to do.

An orange fault light is on. What now?

Usually you can ride on carefully, but have the bike diagnosed soon. If it runs differently than normal, stop.

Can you diagnose my motorcycle?

Yes, where that is possible for your make and model. See diagnostics and fault finding.

What does it cost?

You pay for the time: labour costs €75 per hour, incl. VAT. We keep you informed.
