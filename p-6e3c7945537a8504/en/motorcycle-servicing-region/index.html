OG MotoWorks · Waalwijk

Motorcycle servicing for Kaatsheuvel, Drunen, Dongen, Den Bosch and Oosterhout

We have one workshop, in Waalwijk. Riders from Kaatsheuvel, Drunen, Dongen, Den Bosch and Oosterhout come to us for servicing, repairs, diagnostics and tyres. Your bike comes to our workshop; we work by appointment. Labour costs €75 per hour incl. VAT and we tell you up front what to expect.

Where is our workshop?

Our workshop is at Professor Zeemanweg 6-10 in Waalwijk. That is our only address. We have no branches in other towns and we don't do home visits. Many riders from the surrounding towns do come to us.

How far is it from your town?

Kaatsheuvel and Loon op Zand

Kaatsheuvel is right next to Waalwijk, on the N261. For a service, new tyres or a fault you are with us in no time.

Drunen and Heusden

From Drunen you ride to Waalwijk along the A59. Riders from Drunen, Heusden and Vlijmen come to us for servicing and repairs.

Dongen

Riders from Dongen are also welcome at our workshop in Waalwijk, for a minor or major service, tyres or diagnostics.

Den Bosch ('s-Hertogenbosch)

From Den Bosch you ride to Waalwijk along the A59. Looking for servicing outside the dealer? See our makes page.

Oosterhout

From Oosterhout you reach Waalwijk via the A59. Plan your visit by appointment, so there is time ready for your bike.

Live in Tilburg? We have a separate page for that: motorcycle servicing for riders from Tilburg.

What do we do for you?

  • Minor and major service following your manufacturer's schedule.
  • Repairs of leaks, brakes, chain and more.
  • Diagnostics and fault finding.
  • Motorcycle tyres: advice and fitting, €50 per tyre (tyre not included).
  • Pre-purchase inspection before you buy a motorcycle.

How do we work?

We work by appointment. Message us on WhatsApp to book. Message us your registration number, make, model and mileage. We give you an estimate in advance, and if we find something extra during the work, we consult you first. Labour costs €75 per hour, incl. VAT. See also our prices and our motorcycle garage in Waalwijk.

Frequently asked questions

Do you also come to Kaatsheuvel, Drunen, Dongen, Den Bosch or Oosterhout?

We work in our workshop in Waalwijk. Riders from all these towns are welcome there.

Do you work on all makes?

Yes, we work on motorcycles of all makes. Read more on servicing outside the dealer.

What does an hour of work cost?

Labour costs €75 per hour, incl. VAT. Tyre fitting costs €50 per tyre (tyre not included).

How do I book?

We work by appointment. Message us on WhatsApp to book. Include your registration number, make, model and mileage.
