Motorcycle diagnostics, also on a BMW without a dealer

Having your bike diagnosed helps with a warning light or a bike that behaves strangely. A fault code points to a system or sensor, but is not always the solution straight away. That is why we then measure ourselves. It can also be done outside the dealer, for example on a BMW. Labour costs €75 per hour incl. VAT.

By Freddy · 20 October 2026

When should you have your bike diagnosed?

  • A fault or warning light is on.
  • Your bike runs unevenly, hesitates or stalls.
  • It starts badly.
  • Something electrical behaves strangely, such as the dashboard or the lights.
  • A fault keeps coming back.

What do diagnostics tell you, and what not?

With the diagnostic tool we see the fault codes the bike has stored. Such a code points to a system or sensor, but not always to the real cause. A sensor fault code can also come from a loose connector or a weak battery, for example. That is why we then measure and check ourselves, before any parts are replaced.

Can you have a BMW diagnosed without a dealer?

You can also bring your BMW to us for diagnostics. An example from our workshop: on a BMW R1200GS Adventure the dashboard no longer showed neutral. After connecting the diagnostic tool, resetting the shift values and re-teaching the gear positions, everything worked again.

And other makes?

Whether we can run diagnostics on your bike depends on make, model and year. Older bikes without electronics have nothing to connect to; on those we find faults the classic way. Message us your make, model and year and you know in advance what we can do. See also servicing outside the dealer.

What does it cost?

You pay for the time: labour costs €75 per hour, incl. VAT. We keep you informed, so you know where you stand. More on diagnostics and fault finding in Waalwijk and warning light.

Need help?

We work by appointment. Message us on WhatsApp to book. Include your registration number, make, model and mileage. Our workshop is in Waalwijk; labour costs €75 per hour, incl. VAT.
