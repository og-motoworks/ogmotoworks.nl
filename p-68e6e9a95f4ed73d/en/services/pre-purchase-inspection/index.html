Motorcycle pre-purchase inspection

Buying a used motorcycle? With a pre-purchase inspection we check it first in our workshop in Waalwijk: brakes, tyres, chain, leaks, electrics and a test ride where possible. We tell you what the bike needs, so you can negotiate better. It costs €75 per inspection.

Why a pre-purchase inspection?

With a used bike you don't automatically know the state of the brakes, tyres or chain. With a pre-purchase inspection we take an independent look before you buy. That helps you negotiate, or walk away in time.

What do we check?

  • Brakes: pads, discs and brake fluid.
  • Tyres: tread, damage and age (DOT code).
  • Chain and sprockets: wear and tension.
  • Leaks: oil, coolant, fuel and the forks.
  • Signs of a drop: levers, footrests, fairing parts and the handlebar.
  • Starting and running: cold start, idle and throttle response.
  • Lights, battery and electrics.
  • Diagnostics of the fault memory, where possible for this make and model.

What do you get from us?

We go through what we found with you: what is fine, what needs attention soon and what needs doing straight away. So you know what is still coming after the purchase.

What is a pre-purchase inspection not?

A pre-purchase inspection is a snapshot. It is not a warranty, not a valuation and not an official inspection. We look at what we can see, measure and test in the workshop. We don't take the bike apart.

How does it work?

  • Message us the make, model, year and mileage, and the advert if you have it.
  • We schedule an appointment. We work by appointment. Message us on WhatsApp to book.
  • The bike comes to our workshop in Waalwijk. Agree this with the seller beforehand.
  • We go through the checklist and discuss the result with you.

What does it cost?

A pre-purchase inspection costs €75 per inspection, incl. VAT. Allow about 1 to 1.5 hours. Read also how many km between services and when to replace your tyres.

Frequently asked questions about the pre-purchase inspection

What does a pre-purchase inspection cost?

A pre-purchase inspection costs €75 per inspection, incl. VAT. Allow about 1 to 1.5 hours.

Where does the inspection take place?

At our workshop at Professor Zeemanweg 6-10 in Waalwijk. Agree with the seller that the bike is brought there.
