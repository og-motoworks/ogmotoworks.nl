Crash damage repair

Dropped it, slid out or got hit? We look at the damage, replace what's broken and make sure you can get back on the road safely.

What we replace or repair

  • Brake and clutch levers that are bent or snapped.
  • Footrests and gear or brake pedal.
  • Mirrors and indicators.
  • Fairing parts and other panels that are cracked or broken off.
  • Other damaged parts after a fall or collision.

Also checking what you can't see right away

After a fall, the damage isn't always only on the outside. We also check whether the handlebars are straight, whether brakes, controls and lights work properly and that nothing is leaking. If we see something, we discuss it with you first.

How it works

Send us photos of the damage via WhatsApp, with your make, model and year. We'll then tell you which parts are needed and what you can expect. We charge parts at list price and labour at our hourly rate.

After an accident, through your insurance?

Is the damage going through your insurance, or is your bike at risk of being a total loss? Read what to do about motorcycle damage after a crash or fall.

We help riders from Waalwijk and the region, up to about 50 km from the workshop: from Sprang-Capelle, Kaatsheuvel and Drunen to Tilburg, 's-Hertogenbosch, Oosterhout and Breda.
