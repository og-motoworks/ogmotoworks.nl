Get your motorcycle winter-ready: how to do it

Not riding much in winter? Connect the battery to a trickle charger, fill the tank with a fuel stabiliser, lubricate the chain and set the tyre pressure. Ideally change the oil before winter. That way your bike will start without problems in spring. Want help? See our winter packages.

By Freddy · 20 October 2026

What do you do with the battery?

A battery slowly drains when the bike is ridden little. Connect it to a trickle charger, and your bike will start without problems in spring.

What do you do with the tank and the oil?

Fill the tank and add a fuel stabiliser, so the fuel ages less. Ideally change the oil before winter: old oil contains dirt and acids.

What else do you check?

  • Chain: clean, lubricate and check the slack.
  • Tyres: at the right pressure, and check tread and DOT age.
  • Coolant: is the frost protection right (on liquid-cooled bikes)?
  • Brakes and lights: does everything still work properly?

What do you do in spring?

Before your first ride, check tyre pressure, brakes and chain. Also read our guide motorcycle maintenance: what and when.

Frequently asked questions

Does the battery have to come out?

No need. Connect the battery to a trickle charger, so it stays charged and your bike starts without problems in spring.

Should I change the oil before or after winter?

Ideally before winter, so there is clean oil in your engine.
