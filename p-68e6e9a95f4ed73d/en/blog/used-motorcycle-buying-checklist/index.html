Buying a used motorcycle: what to look for?

With a used motorcycle you first check the papers and service history, then tyres, brakes, chain, leaks and how it starts from cold. Take a test ride if you can. Not sure? Have us check the bike first with a pre-purchase inspection in Waalwijk; the price is on request.

By Freddy · 20 October 2026

Are the papers in order?

  • Compare the registration number and frame number with the papers.
  • Do a free registration check on the RDW website.
  • Ask for invoices and the service book: when was the last service?

What do you check on the bike?

  • Tyres: tread, cracks and the DOT code. Read how old motorcycle tyres may be.
  • Brakes: pad thickness, smooth discs and a firm lever.
  • Chain and sprockets: slack, rust and pointed teeth.
  • Leaks: oil under the bike or on the forks.
  • Damage: scratches on levers, footrests and fairing often point to a fall.
  • Cold start: ask for the bike to be cold when you come to look, and listen whether it runs smoothly.

What do you look for on the test ride?

Pay attention to how it shifts, brakes and rides in a straight line, and whether any lights come on. Only take a test ride if you are insured and the seller agrees.
