What does a major or minor motorcycle service cost?

There is no single fixed price for a service: it depends on your bike, the schedule and the parts. We can explain how the price is built up, so you know where you stand.

What the price consists of

The price of a service has two parts: labour and parts. At OG MotoWorks labour costs €75 per hour, incl. VAT. We charge parts at list price.

Why one service costs more than another

  • Minor or major service: a major service adds parts and work, such as spark plugs, air filter, brake fluid or checking the valves.
  • Your bike: on one bike everything is easy to reach, on another fairing parts or the tank have to come off first.
  • Your manufacturer's schedule: it decides what needs doing at this mileage or after this time.
  • What we find: if we spot something that needs attention during the service, we consult you first. We never do extra work without your approval.

Why we don't quote a fixed price

A fixed price for every bike would be too high for one and too low for another. That is why we look at your bike and give you an estimate in advance.

How you know in advance what it will be

Message us your make, model, year and mileage. We check which service is due and what it includes, and let you know in advance what to expect.
