Motorcycle servicing for riders from Tilburg

Do you live in Tilburg and are you looking for a motorcycle workshop for servicing, repairs or tyres? You are very welcome at OG MotoWorks in Waalwijk.

From Tilburg to our workshop in Waalwijk

OG MotoWorks has no branch in Tilburg. Our workshop is at Professor Zeemanweg 6-10 in Waalwijk. That is our only address. From Tilburg it is an easy ride along the N261. Riders from Tilburg, Tilburg-Noord and Berkel-Enschot already come to us for their service, a repair or new tyres.

What can you come to us for?

  • Motorcycle servicing: minor and major service following your manufacturer's schedule.
  • Motorcycle repairs: leaks, brakes, drive and starting or running problems.
  • Diagnostics and fault finding: when a light is on or it doesn't run right.
  • Motorcycle tyres: advice and fitting for €50 per tyre (tyre not included).
  • Pre-purchase inspection: a second pair of eyes before you buy a motorcycle.
  • Servicing outside the dealer: BMW, Ducati, KTM and other makes.

Why riders from Tilburg come to Waalwijk

With us you have personal contact with Freddy, the mechanic who actually works on your bike. We find the cause first, before any parts are replaced, and we never do extra work without consulting you. You know in advance what it will be.

How to arrange it

  • Message us your registration number, make, model and mileage, and what needs doing.
  • We schedule an appointment. We work by appointment. Message us on WhatsApp to book.
  • You bring your bike to Waalwijk and when you collect it we tell you what we did.

Prices

Labour costs €75 per hour, incl. VAT. Tyre fitting costs €50 per tyre (tyre not included) and we replace fork seals from €350. All prices are on our prices page. Live in a town around Waalwijk? See also motorcycle servicing in the region.

Frequently asked questions from Tilburg

Do you have a workshop in Tilburg?

No. OG MotoWorks has one workshop, at Professor Zeemanweg 6-10 in Waalwijk. Riders from Tilburg are very welcome there.

How do I get to you from Tilburg?

Take the N261 from Tilburg to Waalwijk. Open the route in Google Maps.

What does servicing cost?

Labour costs €75 per hour, incl. VAT. The total price depends on your bike, the service schedule and the parts. Message us your make, model and mileage and you'll know in advance where you stand.

Can I just drop by?

We work by appointment. Message us on WhatsApp to book. That way we have time ready for your bike and you don't make the trip for nothing.
