Motorcycle servicing outside the dealer: BMW, Ducati, KTM and more

You don't have to go to the dealer for servicing. At OG MotoWorks in Waalwijk we work on motorcycles of all makes, following your manufacturer's service schedule.

All makes, one workshop

We work on European and Japanese motorcycles: naked bikes, sports bikes, touring bikes, adventure bikes and older bikes with carburettors. Think BMW, Ducati, KTM, Triumph, Honda, Yamaha, Kawasaki, Suzuki and other makes.

BMW

You can also come to us with your BMW for servicing and repairs. An example from our workshop: on a BMW R1200GS Adventure the dashboard no longer showed neutral. We connected the diagnostic tool, reset the shift values and re-taught the gear positions. After that the dashboard showed everything correctly again.

Ducati, KTM and other European makes

For Ducati, KTM, Triumph and other European makes we also follow the manufacturer's service schedule. What your service includes depends on your model and mileage. Send us your make, model, year and mileage, we look it up and you know in advance what to expect.

Japanese makes

Honda, Yamaha, Kawasaki and Suzuki: from a modern fuel-injected bike to an older bike with carburettors. We also clean and synchronise carburettors, see tuning and adjustment.

Honest about what we do and don't do

  • We follow your manufacturer's service schedule and tell you what we did.
  • Whether we can run diagnostics on your bike depends on make, model and year. We tell you honestly in advance.
  • Is your bike still under manufacturer's warranty? Then first read your make's warranty terms or check them with your dealer.
  • We don't do paintwork or bodywork, and we only work on motorcycles, not on scooters or mopeds.

What does it cost?

Labour costs €75 per hour, incl. VAT. We charge parts at list price, used parts by agreement. Read also what a major or minor service costs and motorcycle servicing in Waalwijk. We work by appointment. Message us on WhatsApp to book.

Frequently asked questions about servicing outside the dealer

Do I have to go to the dealer for servicing?

No, you can have your bike serviced where you like. If your bike is still under manufacturer's warranty, first read your make's warranty terms or check them with your dealer.

Do you follow my manufacturer's schedule?

Yes. We follow the service schedule the manufacturer prescribes for your model and tell you what we did.

Can you run diagnostics on my bike?

On many bikes, yes. We tell you in advance whether it is possible for your make, model and year. Message us your make, model and year.

What does it cost?

Labour costs €75 per hour, incl. VAT. We charge parts at list price.
