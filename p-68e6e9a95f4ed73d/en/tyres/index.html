Frequently asked questions about motorcycle tyres

When should I replace my motorcycle tyres?

When the tread has worn down to the wear indicators (legal minimum in the Netherlands: 1 mm), when the rubber shows cracks or is hard and old, or with a cut or bulge. Read when to replace your tyres.

How can I see how old my tyre is?

From the DOT code on the sidewall. The last four digits give the production week and year: 2423 is week 24 of 2023.

What does tyre fitting cost?

Tyre fitting costs €50 per tyre, incl. VAT. The tyre itself is charged separately.

How do I find the right tyre size?

Choose your bike in the tyre menu, or send us your tyre size. How to read your tyre size.
