Bike hesitating or running rough?

Cutting out when pulling away, a rough idle or poor throttle response: your bike isn't running the way it should.

Possible causes

  • A dirty air filter or worn spark plugs.
  • On older bikes: dirty carburettors or carburettors that aren't in sync.
  • An air leak at an intake rubber.
  • A fault in a sensor or in the ignition. Is a light on too? See warning light on.
  • Old or wrong fuel, for example after standing still for a long time.

What we do

We start with the basics: filters, spark plugs and the service history. Then we keep searching, and where needed we clean and synchronise the carburettors or set up the bike again. See also tuning & adjustment and diagnostics.
