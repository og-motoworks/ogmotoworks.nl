Tuning & adjustment

Is your bike no longer running as smoothly as you're used to? With adjustment and fine-tuning we get it running sharp and even again.

Noticing this?

  • Your bike idles roughly or stalls.
  • It hesitates or cuts out when pulling away.
  • It vibrates more than normal or the cylinders aren't running evenly.
  • It uses noticeably more fuel than you're used to.

What we do

  • Cleaning and synchronising carburettors, so all cylinders run evenly.
  • Adjusting and fine-tuning things like idle speed and throttle response.
  • Finding the cause if it keeps hesitating; see also diagnostics & faults.

Price

Tell us what you're noticing and we'll give you an estimate of the time up front.

We help riders from Waalwijk and the region, up to about 50 km from the workshop: from Sprang-Capelle, Kaatsheuvel and Drunen to Tilburg, 's-Hertogenbosch, Oosterhout and Breda.
