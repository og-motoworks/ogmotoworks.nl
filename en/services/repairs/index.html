Repairs

Something leaking, a strange noise or something that just doesn't feel right? We find out what it is and fix it, with honest advice about what really needs doing.

What you can come to us for

  • Oil leaks, leaking gaskets and seals.
  • Brakes: pads, discs, brake fluid and calipers.
  • Chain, sprockets and drive (see chain and sprockets).
  • Electrical problems and faults (see diagnostics).
  • Punctured or damaged tyres (see tyres).
  • Forks that leak or sag (see fork seals).

Repair where we can

At the dealer, parts are often replaced straight away at full price. We first check whether a part can be repaired, whether a good second-hand part will do, and what you really need. We discuss the options with you upfront.

Cost

For some jobs the price is fixed upfront: fork seals €350 fixed and tyre fitting €50 per tyre (tyre extra), all incl. VAT. A chain kit is priced on quotation. Extra work always after consultation.

We help riders from Waalwijk and the region, up to about 50 km from the workshop: from Sprang-Capelle, Kaatsheuvel and Drunen to Tilburg, 's-Hertogenbosch, Oosterhout and Breda.
