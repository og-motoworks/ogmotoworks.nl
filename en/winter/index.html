Terms

Book by 30 November 2026

You can book from 12 October to 30 November 2026. We carry out the packages until 20 December 2026. The spring check of Winterklaar Complete is planned in March or April 2027.

  • All prices incl. 21% VAT. Fixed price for what is in the package.
  • More than 4 litres of oil, a different oil specification or an expensive or hard-to-reach filter? We tell you the extra cost in advance.
  • If we find something during the check, we first send you a photo and a price via WhatsApp. We only go ahead after your OK. Our hourly rate for extra work is €75 incl. VAT.
  • Tyres, batteries and repairs are by quotation.
  • Coolant only for liquid-cooled bikes; a full coolant replacement is by quotation.
  • Motorcycles only: no scooters or mopeds.
  • You can cancel free of charge up to 24 hours in advance via WhatsApp.
  • Cannot be combined with other offers or discounts.

You bring your bike to our workshop in Waalwijk and take it home again the same evening or day. By appointment only.
