Privacy statement

Short and clear: which data we use, what for, and what your rights are.

OG MotoWorks, Professor Zeemanweg 6-10, 5144 NN Waalwijk, the Netherlands (Chamber of Commerce/KvK 90649443), handles your data with care. Here you can read which data we use and why.

Which data?

  • What you fill in yourself in the request form: name, mobile number, email (optional), registration number (optional), mileage (optional), preferred day (optional), your bike and what you want done.
  • Whether you want to receive a review request by email (yes or no).
  • What you send us via WhatsApp, email or phone.

What for?

Only to handle your request: contacting you, giving you a price and planning, carrying out the work on your bike and invoicing. We don't sell your data and don't use it for advertising.

Via WhatsApp

The form prepares your details in a WhatsApp message. Only when you press send in WhatsApp does that message reach us. WhatsApp is a service of Meta; WhatsApp's privacy policy also applies.

Copy by email (Formspree)

When you press Open WhatsApp, your request is also sent to us by email. That way nothing gets lost, even if the WhatsApp message isn't sent. For this we use Formspree, an American service that processes the data only on our behalf (processor). Your data may be processed in the United States. Formspree uses the Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) of the European Commission for this. Formspree keeps the submission in its archive for up to 30 days. After that, your request is only in our own email.

Your email address

Your email address is not required. We only use it if you fill it in: to email you a quote or your invoice. Only if you tick the box will we email you once after the job to ask for a review. You can withdraw your consent for the review request at any time by sending us a WhatsApp message or an email to info@ogmotoworks.nl. After that we will not send you a review request.

On your own device

The site remembers your chosen bike in your browser (localStorage), so you don't have to choose it again every time. This stays on your own device; you can delete it with the button above or by clearing your browser data. What you fill in on the request form (including your name, mobile number and email) is only kept in this tab's session storage (sessionStorage), so it isn't lost when you go to another page. Close the tab and it's gone.

Visitor counter

We count visits with GoatCounter. It works without cookies and without personal data.

How long?

We don't keep your data longer than needed for your request and the work on your bike. We keep invoices for 7 years, because the law requires it.

Your rights

You may view, correct or delete your data. Email us at info@ogmotoworks.nl or message us. If you disagree with how we handle your data, you can file a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).
